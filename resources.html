Electrocardio Panorama

A research series on continuous-view ECG reconstruction, with two complementary data resources: the original Nef-Net release and Panobench from NEF-NET v2.

Original Release · Nef-Net

IJCAI 2021

Lead geometry and waveform annotations for continuous-view ECG research.

12-lead reference angles30 CT scansCycle & deflection labels

The paper quantifies standard 12-lead viewpoints using average angles measured from 30 body CT scans, and releases cardiac-cycle and waveform-deflection annotations for Tianchi ECG and PTB. The repository includes lead-angle mappings, annotation tools, and the Nef-Net implementation.

The distinctive contribution is reusable spatial and temporal supervision: lead identity becomes an explicit viewing direction, while waveform segments support structured modeling of each heartbeat.

The angles are shared lead-level geometric priors, not individually measured positions for every participant. Original ECG signals are obtained from their source datasets.

Panobench · NEF-NET v2

ICLR 2026

Dense ECG viewpoints with explicit spatial geometry.

48 views4,470 recordings10 seconds eachCT-derived angles

Six limb leads and 42 precordial leads provide much denser spatial coverage than a standard 12-lead ECG. Each viewpoint is annotated with CT-derived spherical coordinates (θ, φ), linking the recorded waveform to its observation direction.

This pairing makes Panobench a benchmark for geometry-aware ECG modeling, sparse-to-dense reconstruction, and synthesis at held-out viewpoints: predictions can be compared with recorded signals at the target views.
