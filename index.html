JINTAI CHEN Artificial Intelligence · Health · Science

Machine Learning Foundations and Biomedical Intelligence

My research develops AI systems that learn from heterogeneous data, generate useful scientific objects and hypotheses, and reason over structured evidence.

The goal is to connect methodological advances in artificial intelligence with real biomedical problems— from therapeutic discovery and clinical development to diagnosis and treatment.

Biomedical Focus

Rather than treating each disease or modality as a separate research identity, I organize biomedical work along a translational continuum.
