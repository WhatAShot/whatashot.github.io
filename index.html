About

AI researcher working across methods, science, and medicine.

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor at HKUST(GZ), affiliated with HKUST, and PI of the ML4H Lab. Before joining HKUST(GZ), I was a postdoctoral researcher in Computer Science at UIUC.

I received my PhD in Computer Science and Technology from Zhejiang University. My broader goal is to build AI systems that remain technically ambitious while being useful in real scientific and clinical settings.
